Black Holes

What is a black hole?

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is strong enough that, beyond its event horizon, signals cannot escape to distant observers.

Key formula

rₛ = 2GM/c²

For a non-rotating, uncharged black hole, this is the Schwarzschild radius.

Questions to explore

How does light behave near a horizon? What changes for rotating black holes? How does quantum field theory in curved spacetime lead to Hawking radiation?
